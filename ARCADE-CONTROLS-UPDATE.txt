DARKNOTE ARCADE CONTROLS UPDATE

Open DARKNOTE_PHONE_ARCADE.html in a modern browser.

- Offline Run is black and white and playable without network assets. Tap the stage or Jump button to hop over cacti.
- Snake board is smaller, with directional controls underneath.
- Car Rush auto-accelerates; use Left and Right to steer. Speed rises with levels.
- Ninja Rush uses a single Jump control plus Start/Restart.
- Other mini-games and the phone-style Menu2 shell are preserved.

Standalone HTML arcade prototype; it does not replace the WhatsApp bot runtime or command handlers.
